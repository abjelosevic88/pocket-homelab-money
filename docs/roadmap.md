# Roadmap

Ideas, roughly in order:

- **Receipt reading** — photo → amount, date, shop and category via the AI key, for receipts without a fiscal QR code (fiscal QR codes are read already).
- **More fiscal countries** — Federation of BiH once it has QR verification, North Macedonia, Slovenia; each is one `internal/fiscal` provider.
- **Bank sync** — GoCardless / Nordigen or Enable Banking for banks that offer it; CSV until then.
- **Offline quick add** — queue entries in the PWA without a connection and send them later.
- **Pocket Split hand-off** — “split this” on a transaction creates the shared expense in Pocket Split and books only your share.
- **Investments** — holdings with prices for the investment account type.
- **Shared budgets** — a budget over accounts shared with someone else.
- **Year in review** — a printable/PDF summary.
